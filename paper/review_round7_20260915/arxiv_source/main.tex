\title{Beyond Mean Foils: Auditing Worst-Foil Specificity in Frozen CLIP Region Explanations}

\begin{abstract}
Spatial overlap and mean class contrast need not imply target-specific region
responses. We audit CCI's maximum-target-drop region in frozen CLIP, using
separate prompts for selection and evaluation. Across COCO and VOC with two
checkpoints, 41.16--64.78\% of locality/mean-foil screen-passers fail a worst-foil
criterion. With original screening memberships retained, excluding annotated-present
foils leaves 39.77--63.64\% failing. Exclusion reduces failure beyond exact
cardinality-matched random removal by 0.86--0.92 percentage points on COCO;
VOC intervals include zero. Positive-target filtering retains high failure.
Among screened failures, unrestricted eight-region oracles permit sign repair
in 6.25--7.84\% on COCO and 27.40--31.29\% on VOC. A target-drop tolerance
of $\epsilon=.02$ reduces feasible repair to 0.16--0.98\%; relaxing it increases
opportunity with setting-dependent target and locality changes.
\end{abstract}
\begin{keywords}
CLIP, explanation auditing, class specificity, constrained region selection
\end{keywords}

\section{Introduction}
CLIP~\cite{radford2021clip} region explanations use intervention-induced
image--text score changes. Cluster-based Concept Importance
(CCI)~\cite{agarwal2025cci} combines cluster scores into a heatmap;
we audit only its maximum-target-drop region, CCI-top1. Spatial overlap,
positive target contribution, and target specificity are distinct:
a region may support multiple genuinely present classes.

COCOA~\cite{lin2023cocoa} studies corpus--foil attribution;
CASE~\cite{williamson2025case} builds contrastive saliency maps.
Related CLIP attribution~\cite{wang2023m2ib,li2023clipsurgery,dreyer2025cliplatent},
class contrasts~\cite{li2026cdaclip},
and caption foils~\cite{yuksekgonul2023aro} motivate our audit.
Our contribution is a controlled empirical audit: we measure the
mean--worst-foil gap within a declared screen, compare annotation exclusion
against exact cardinality-matched removal, and decompose candidate, budget,
and selector limits. Worst-foil (WF) reranking is a diagnostic probe;
unlike IDEA's few-shot adaptation~\cite{ye2026idea}, CLIP stays fixed.

\section{Audit and Repair Opportunity}
For checkpoint $m$, the pre-softmax score is
$s_m(x,c)=\tau_m\langle z_m(x),e_m(c)\rangle$, with unit-norm embeddings
and learned $\tau_m=\exp(\ell_m)$. Region $R$ has intervention response
\begin{equation}
d_R(c)=s_m(x,c)-s_m(\mathcal I(x,R),c).
\label{eq:drop}
\end{equation}
For target category $t$, selection uses ``a photo of a \{category\}'':
\begin{equation}
R_{\rm CCI}=\arg\max_{R\in\regions(x)}d_R^{\rm sel}(t).
\label{eq:cci}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Held-out margins and screens.}
Evaluation uses ``a picture of the \{category\}'', ``an image containing a
\{category\}'', and ``the \{category\}''. For unit-norm text embeddings
$e_h(c)$, let $H=3$, $n_c=\|H^{-1}\sum_h e_h(c)\|_2$, and
$\bar d_c=H^{-1}\sum_h d_h(c)/n_c$. With $\foils_t$ the non-target dataset
categories, the aggregate worst-foil margin is
\begin{equation}
P(R,t)=\bar d_t-\max_{f\in\foils_t}\bar d_f.
\label{eq:estimands}
\end{equation}
This equals using normalized mean text embeddings; class-specific $n_c$ can
change rankings. Tables report raw target drop $H^{-1}\sum_h d_h(t)$,
whereas the positive-target screen uses $\bar d_t$. Evaluation templates
are not used to select a region.

For CCI-top1, define
$A=\{\mathrm{bbox}\ge .5,\ \bar d_t-\mathrm{mean}_f\bar d_f>0\}$ and
$F=\{P<0\}$. The diagnostic screen and $.5$ threshold are study-defined,
not a standard of explanation validity. Because a positive mean-foil margin
does not require positive target drop, we also audit
$A^+=A\cap\{\bar d_t>0\}$. The primary repair population is
$B=A\cap F$, fixed before reranking. Here ``failure'' means failure of this declared criterion, not a semantic
error or an invalid explanation.

\paragraph{Target-constrained diagnostic probe.}
Define
\begin{equation}
M^{\rm sel}(R,t)=d_R^{\rm sel}(t)-\max_{f\in\foils_t}d_R^{\rm sel}(f),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\regions_\epsilon=\{R:d_R^{\rm sel}(t)\ge d_{R_{\rm CCI}}^{\rm sel}(t)-\epsilon\},
\label{eq:feasible}
\end{equation}
and worst-foil (WF) reranking
\begin{equation}
R_{\rm WF}=\arg\max_{R\in\regions_\epsilon}M^{\rm sel}(R,t).
\label{eq:wf}
\end{equation}
The baseline is feasible, so any switch loses at most $\epsilon$ of
\emph{selection-stage} target drop; this gives no held-out guarantee.
Mean and Max-.1 instead maximize $d_t^{\rm sel}-\mathrm{mean}_f d_f^{\rm sel}$
and $d_t^{\rm sel}-.1\max_f d_f^{\rm sel}$ over the same set.
The reference budget is $\epsilon=.02$ in logit units, not $2\%$ relative
loss. A post-hoc $.01/.02/.05/.10/.20$ sweep selects no new optimum.
Cosine budgets $\epsilon/\tau_m$ are not calibrated across checkpoints;
unrestricted oracles remove the budget entirely.

\paragraph{Exact diagnostic oracles.}
For a baseline-failure population $B$, the feasible repair count is
\begin{equation}
N_O(B,\epsilon)=\sum_{i\in B}\mathbf1\!\left\{
\max_{R\in\regions_\epsilon(i)}P_i(R)\ge0\right\}.
\label{eq:oracle}
\end{equation}
The unrestricted oracle searches all $K$ candidates. A sign pass means
$P_i(R)\ge0$, without locality or positive-target constraints. Failures split
into: no passing candidate ($C_0$); passing candidates but \emph{none feasible}
($C_1$); a feasible pass missed by WF ($C_2$); and WF sign repair ($r$).
Thus $|B|=C_0+C_1+C_2+r$ and $N_O=C_2+r$. We additionally count repairs
satisfying $J(R)=\{P_i(R)\ge0,\ A(R),\ \bar d_t(R)>0\}$; $A(R)$ applies
the same full-foil screen to the new region, without changing original $B$.
Oracles use held-out data only for diagnosis.

\paragraph{Fixed-population foil controls.}
For image $i$, let $\foils_i^{\rm abs}$ remove all annotated-present categories
from the non-target foils. The protocol holds $R_{\rm CCI}$ and full-foil screen $A$ fixed;
$F_i^{\rm abs}$ tests $\bar d_t-\max_{f\in\foils_i^{\rm abs}}\bar d_f<0$.
No region or screen is reselected. Reducing the foil set cannot increase
failure, so we also match its cardinality. For $N_i$ full
foils, $k_i=|\foils_i^{\rm abs}|$, and $m_i$ foils exceeding the target,
a uniform size-$k_i$ subset without replacement fails with probability
\begin{equation}
q_i=1-\frac{\binom{N_i-m_i}{k_i}}{\binom{N_i}{k_i}}.
\label{eq:matched}
\end{equation}
With zero numerator when $k_i>N_i-m_i$, we compare
$\Delta_A=|A|^{-1}\sum_{i\in A}(\mathbf1\{F_i^{\rm abs}\}-q_i)$.
Restricted sets are nonempty. \mbox{Annotation} absence does not establish semantic
absence; crowd annotations remain excluded.

\section{Protocol and Reproducibility}
COCO val2014~\cite{lin2014coco} and VOC2007 test~\cite{everingham2010voc}
provide 27,708/1,943 eligible images, each evaluated with OpenAI CLIP B/16
and B/32 (59,302 image--model records). The target is the largest instance's
category, using annotation area on COCO and box area on VOC. We require
another category and 2--80\% target area; COCO crowd annotations are excluded.
Full foils are the other 79/19 classes.

K-means clusters L2-normalized projected patch features: $K=8$, three
initializations, 50 iterations, seed $1701+i$ (sorted IDs shuffled with seed
1701 define $i$). Intervention $\mathcal I$
adds $-\infty$ to attention logits at selected patch-key columns before
softmax in every block/head, retaining the class-token column. Ties use first argmax; MPS features are cast to float32.
BBox precision is the fraction of selected patch centers in target-class box unions,
mapped by $224/\min(W,H)$ and center-crop offsets; it measures neither pixel
precision nor target coverage~\cite{gomez2022metrics}.

Float32 candidate analyses use stored responses. Foil controls regenerate
full-class responses: IDs, CCI labels, feasible sizes, and full-foil states
match; compared summaries differ by less than $10^{-5}$. Original $A,A^+$
memberships are retained. This verifies summaries, not
spatial identity: original masks are unavailable. Region-fixed comparisons
are conditional on faithful regeneration.

Intervals use 10,000 image bootstraps and
percentile 2.5/97.5 limits, pointwise without multiplicity correction.
Paired quantities are differenced within image before resampling. Foil
comparisons resample within fixed $A/A^+$; repair-cost intervals condition
on actual repairs. Exact $q_i$ is primary; 100 seeded draws check it.

\paragraph{Budget relaxation helps in every setting.}
At $.02$, 95.21--95.60\% of images have only one feasible candidate
(Table~\ref{tab:frontier}); WF has little room to change the region.
At $.20$, oracle repair rises to 1.57/2.05/6.65/7.13\% of fixed $B$.
Raw-target changes become negative in all four settings, but bbox changes
are negative on COCO and positive on VOC. Thus relaxation does not imply
uniform locality loss. These post-hoc logit-budget comparisons are
within-setting diagnostics, not calibrated cross-model rankings.

\paragraph{Global impact is not the cost of repair.}
Table~\ref{tab:frontier} includes unchanged regions. In contrast,

\paragraph{Sign repair need not retain the other endpoints.}
Only 12/25/3/1 of the 18/30/5/2 repairs in $B$ also retain the original

feasible-oracle counts are 12/26/3/2. Small VOC repair subsets make their

Across \emph{all baseline failures}, WF sign-repairs 42/48/11/6 cases;
0/4/0/0 opposite-sign regressions leave net reductions of 42/44/11/6.

B/16 bbox changes of $-4.95/-13.20$ points on COCO/VOC.
These are different populations from $B$ repairs. Raw-target intervals
containing zero do not establish equivalence.

\paragraph{Failure is not solely a near-zero effect.}
At $\delta=.10$, 59.49/60.22\% of COCO and 36.23/34.47\% of VOC
screen-passers still fail (Table~\ref{tab:threshold}). Rates fall further at
$.50$, so the magnitude and threshold matter. This checks sign-boundary
sensitivity within the existing normalized score, not sensitivity to
class-dependent normalization or semantic validity.

\section{Interpretation and Limitations}
Matched-cardinality controls separate annotation exclusion from foil-count
reduction within a setting. Missed labels and related concepts can remain
hard foils; negative margins do not establish semantic error. Hardest-foil
inspection and human validation remain necessary.

The audit concerns CCI-top1, eight candidates, and one attention intervention.
The COCO/VOC contrast also changes foil count (79 versus 19), target selection,
and annotation conditions; it cannot isolate intrinsic dataset difficulty.
Within-setting controls do not remove these differences. Other $K$, candidate constructions,
interventions, and area-matched controls remain untested. Bbox precision
measures neither coverage nor segmentation quality.

\paragraph{Reproducibility and normalization.}
\href{https://github.com/jovial-liu/conway/tree/paper/verified-rerun-integration/experiments}{Stored candidate summaries and analysis scripts} support the reported
threshold and budget reanalyses. Original spatial masks and complete
per-class responses are unavailable in these artifacts. Consequently,
summary agreement does not verify original region identity, and raw versus
normalized worst-foil rankings cannot be reconstructed. Prompt holdout
separates templates, not models or images. Exact candidate enumeration
does not resolve these evidence gaps.

\section{Conclusion}
Under a declared worst-foil criterion, substantial failure remains after
annotation-based foil exclusion and after moving the failure threshold away
from zero. This is evidence about the audit criterion, not semantic error.
Eight-candidate enumeration separates missing candidates from budget and
selector limits. Looser budgets increase repair opportunity in every setting,
with setting-dependent endpoint changes. Region identity, normalization,
and foil-ontology controls are needed before broader claims about explanation
reliability or cross-dataset differences.
