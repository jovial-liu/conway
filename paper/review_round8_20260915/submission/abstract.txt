A region can overlap the target object and still contribute more to another class. We study this mismatch in frozen CLIP using regions selected by Cluster-based Concept Importance (CCI). Across COCO and VOC with two checkpoints, 41.08-64.78% of regions that pass overlap and mean-contrast checks fail against the strongest competing class. Removing competitors annotated in the image leaves 39.69-63.64% failing. To understand whether region selection can resolve these failures, we examine all eight candidates per image. An alternative passes the test for 6.25-7.84% of failures on COCO and 27.40-31.15% on VOC. Requiring it to preserve the original target-score drop within epsilon = 0.02 reduces these rates to 0.16-0.98%. The main constraints are therefore the available regions and the target-drop tolerance; relaxing the tolerance increases repair opportunities.
