\title{Beyond Mean Foils: Auditing Worst-Foil Specificity in Frozen CLIP Region Explanations}
\input{authors.tex}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
A frozen CLIP region can pass locality and mean-foil screens while responding
more strongly to a non-target foil. We audit the maximum-target-drop single
CCI region using matched prompt-mean and aggregate worst-foil margins.
In the frozen COCO B/16 archive, 41.18\% of eligible images pass both screens
yet fail the worst-foil audit; 64.38\% [63.67,65.07]\% of screen-passers fail.
Target-tolerant reranking at $\epsilon=.02$ switches 2.0--2.6\% of images;
paired margin gains persist under category-disjoint and annotation-absent foils.
Independent four-setting MPS reruns add direct paired gains over Mean and
Max-.1, but gains over Max-.1 are small. Switched-image bbox precision drops
by 4.95 and 13.20 percentage points on COCO and VOC B/16.
Margin improvement is sparse, differs from sign repair, and does not imply
loss-free explanations.

\end{abstract}
\begin{keywords}
CLIP, explainability, interventions, model auditing, worst-foil specificity
\end{keywords}

\section{Introduction}
Frozen vision-language models such as CLIP~\cite{radford2021clip} are often
interpreted through intervention-induced image--text score changes.
CCI~\cite{agarwal2025cci} clusters patch tokens and combines cluster importance
into a heatmap. We audit only its maximum-target-drop region (CCI-top1).
A large target drop need not be target-specific: generic object evidence can
support another class more strongly, despite high box overlap. Averaging easy
foils can hide this failure. In the frozen COCO B/16 archive, 11,411 of 17,725
locality/mean-class screen-passers (64.38\%) have negative worst-foil margin.

Our contribution is the selected-region response-vector audit and controlled
evidence of local improvement, not the max/CVaR operator~\cite{rockafellar2000cvar}.
COCOA~\cite{lin2023cocoa} defines corpus-versus-foil attribution;
CASE~\cite{williamson2025case} diagnoses class-insensitive saliency and builds
contrastive explanations. We test what locality and mean-class screens miss,
then separate region selection from held-out audit.
Related CLIP attribution~\cite{wang2023m2ib,li2023clipsurgery,dreyer2025cliplatent},
class/concept contrasts~\cite{li2026cdaclip,cheng2026mgaclip,visser2026contrastiveconcept}
and caption foils~\cite{yuksekgonul2023aro} motivate this distinction.
Image Description Enhanced CLIP-Adapter (IDEA)~\cite{ye2026idea} combines visual
features and descriptions for few-shot classification; our audit keeps the
model fixed. Reranking probes local improvement. Negative relative specificity
does not imply absent target evidence: one region may support several classes.

\section{Worst-Foil Audit and Target-Tolerant Reranking}
For checkpoint $m$, $s_m(x,c)=\tau_m\langle z_m(x),e_m(c)\rangle$ uses
unit-normalized embeddings and the checkpoint's learned
$\tau_m=\exp(\mathrm{logit\_scale}_m)$, before softmax.
For candidate region $R$ and concept prompt $c$, define
\begin{equation}
\dropv_R(c)=s_m(x,c)-s_m(\mathcal I(x,R),c),
\label{eq:drop}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal I$ is a frozen internal intervention. Our single-region CCI baseline
selects
\begin{equation}
R_{\rm CCI}=\arg\max_{R\in\regions(x)}\dropv_R^{\rm sel}(t),
\label{eq:cci}
\end{equation}
using only the selection prompt. CCI-top1 denotes this baseline;
the CCI subscripts and figure labels retain this shorthand.
For non-target foils $\foils_t$, a worst-foil
margin compares the target response with the strongest foil response.

\paragraph{Matched held-out estimands.}
Selection uses ``a photo of a \{category\}''. Held-out evaluation uses
``a picture of the \{category\}'', ``an image containing a \{category\}'', and
``the \{category\}''. Let $e_h(c)$ denote the stored unit-norm text embedding
for held-out prompt $h$, and
$n_c=\|H^{-1}\sum_h e_h(c)\|_2$.
We apply the same class-specific normalization rule,
$\widetilde d_h(c)=d_h(c)/n_c$ and~define
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
P_{\max}^{\rm pm}(R,t)
&=H^{-1}\!\sum_h[\widetilde d_h(t)-\max_f\widetilde d_h(f)],\\
P_{\max}^{\rm agg}(R,t)
&=H^{-1}\!\sum_h\widetilde d_h(t)-
\max_f H^{-1}\!\sum_h\widetilde d_h(f).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:estimands}
\end{equation}
Thus $P_{\max}^{\rm pm}\le P_{\max}^{\rm agg}$: the former permits a different
hardest foil per template. Both are ontology-relative specificity measures.
For the mean-class screen, write $\bar d_c=H^{-1}\sum_h\widetilde d_h(c)$.
The archived screen uses $P_{\rm mean,all}=\bar d_t-|\mathcal C|^{-1}\sum_{c\in\mathcal C}\bar d_c$
over all dataset classes, equally weighted, on these same held-out responses.
Excluding the target gives $P_{\rm mean,foil}$, with
$P_{\rm mean,all}=(|\mathcal C|-1)P_{\rm mean,foil}/|\mathcal C|$;
their positive screens coincide. This class average differs from prompt-mean.

\paragraph{Selection-stage target tolerance.}
For each candidate define
\begin{equation}
M^{\rm sel}(R,t)=\dropv_R^{\rm sel}(t)-
\max_{f\in\foils_t}\dropv_R^{\rm sel}(f).
\end{equation}
Given tolerance $\epsilon$, form
\begin{equation}
\regions_\epsilon=\{R:\dropv_R^{\rm sel}(t)\ge
\dropv_{R_{\rm CCI}}^{\rm sel}(t)-\epsilon\},
\end{equation}
and rerank only this feasible set:
\begin{equation}
R_{\rm WF}(\epsilon)=\arg\max_{R\in\regions_\epsilon}M^{\rm sel}(R,t).
\label{eq:repair}
\end{equation}
Because $R_{\rm CCI}\in\regions_\epsilon$, the feasible set is nonempty;
any switch loses at most $\epsilon$ of the \emph{selection-stage} target drop.
This is not a held-out target guarantee. Equation~\ref{eq:repair} reads only
selection-stage tensors, and held-out prompts are used only after the region is
fixed. We use $\epsilon=.02$ as a fixed operating point, not an optimized value.
Its cosine-scale tolerance is $.02/\tau_m$, not necessarily equal across checkpoints.
Figure~\ref{fig:method} summarizes the separation.

\section{Experimental Protocol}
\paragraph{Models, intervention, and endpoints.}
We use $K=8$ frozen CCI regions on COCO val2014~\cite{lin2014coco}
(27,708 eligible images) and VOC2007 test~\cite{everingham2010voc} (1,943), with
OpenAI CLIP B/16 and B/32. Reranking changes only which frozen candidate is
selected. Each image has one target: the largest-area annotated instance's
category (COCO annotation area; VOC box area). Eligibility requires another
annotated category and target-instance area between 2\% and 80\% of the image;
COCO crowd annotations are excluded. Full foils comprise every other dataset
category (79 for COCO, 19 for VOC). The intervention blocks selected patch key/value columns in every
visual-transformer block and head by adding $-\infty$ before attention softmax,
while retaining the prefix token. Cached patch features are clustered by
K-means ($n_{\rm init}=3$, 50 iterations, seed $1701+i$ for cache index $i$).
BBox precision is the fraction of selected patch centers in the union of
transformed boxes of the target category, not only its largest instance, and is
reported separately because perturbation metrics depend on the intervention
operator~\cite{gomez2022metrics}. Ties select the first exact argmax.

\paragraph{Held-out controls and uncertainty.}
Selection uses one template and evaluation uses the three held-out templates in
Eq.~\ref{eq:estimands}. Cross-foil controls use ten fixed seeds
$1701,2701,\ldots,10701$ to permute categories into equal alternating folds.
Each direction selects using one fold and audits on the other, excluding the
target from both. The 20 seed--direction deltas are averaged within each image
before paired unique-image resampling;
annotation-absent evaluation removes categories annotated as present without
claiming semantic absence. Fixed 5/19-foil subsets probe cardinality and composition jointly.
Intervals use 10,000 paired unique-image bootstrap resamples; the archive fixes
the base seed at 1701 and records deterministic setting/metric-specific seeds.
Only four provenance-valid OpenAI settings enter the direct reranking analysis;
two historical LAION settings are excluded because their text-normalization
provenance is unresolved.


\paragraph{Independent local rerun.}
We regenerated candidates and responses on a MacBook M4 Pro using PyTorch 2.8.0
MPS, the same declared OpenAI models, splits, templates, $K=8$ and $\epsilon=.02$.
Tables~\ref{tab:screens}, \ref{tab:baselines} and \ref{tab:repair_accounting}
report this rerun; Table~\ref{tab:main}, foil/tolerance controls and
Figures~\ref{fig:ident}--\ref{fig:cases} retain frozen-archive results.
Rerun intervals are 10,000-draw percentile image bootstraps with recorded
metric-specific seeds. Direct comparisons resample same-image differences;
conditional failure jointly resamples numerator and denominator, and
switch intervals resample switched images. Intervals are pointwise, without
multiple-comparison adjustment.

\paragraph{Worst-foil debt.}
Frozen COCO B/16 CCI-top1 has $P_{\max}^{\rm agg}=-.342$ [--.357,--.327],
raw target response $.939$ [.924,.953] and bbox precision $.679$ [.674,.683].
These are full-sample means. Within its negative-margin subset, mean target
drop is $.481$ and 90.40\% have positive drop. The independent four-setting
audit (Table~\ref{tab:screens}) finds COCO B/16 failures in 11,410/17,726
screen-passers, versus frozen 11,411/17,725.

\paragraph{Sparse margin improvement.}
Frozen Table~\ref{tab:main} shows positive aggregate margin gains in all
settings. Prompt-mean gains are $+.0150,+.0108,+.0119,+.00867$, also with
CIs above zero. All raw target-response CIs include zero, without establishing
equivalence. B/16 bbox declines on COCO and VOC; B/32 intervals include zero.

\paragraph{The gain is not merely selector--endpoint reuse.}
Frozen cross-foil controls (Figure~\ref{fig:ident}) retain positive normalized
aggregate gains: $.01052/.00728/.00705/.00438$ across COCO B/16, COCO B/32,
VOC B/16 and VOC B/32. Annotation-absent evaluation gives
$.00939/.00697/.00809/.00443$. All paired 95\% CIs exceed zero.
These controls establish gain persistence, not restricted-foil baseline
conditional failure rates.

\paragraph{Direct same-feasible-set comparisons.}
Table~\ref{tab:baselines} reports direct rerun WF-minus-alternative differences.
Mean maximizes $d_t^{\rm sel}-\mathrm{mean}_f d_f^{\rm sel}$ and Max-.1 maximizes
$d_t^{\rm sel}-.1\max_f d_f^{\rm sel}$ over the same $\regions_\epsilon$.
All eight pointwise margin CIs exceed zero, but gains over Max-.1 are small.
Relative to Mean, bbox declines on COCO B/16, COCO B/32 and VOC B/16 with
CIs below zero. Relative to Max-.1, bbox CIs include zero in all settings,
while COCO B/16 raw target response decreases by $.000099$
$[-.000192,-.000010]$. These results do not establish loss-free superiority.

\paragraph{Why switching is sparse.}
In the local rerun, singleton feasible sets account for 95.29/95.60/95.21/95.27\%
of COCO B/16, COCO B/32, VOC B/16 and VOC B/32 images.
The counts with 2/3/4 candidates are 1215/81/8, 1174/43/2, 88/5/0 and 89/3/0;
none have more than four. COCO B/16 permits an alternative in 1,304/27,708
images, and 663/1,304 (50.84\%) switch. The frozen counterpart is 1,303.

\paragraph{The effect also reduces the breadth of foil violations.}
As a secondary held-out diagnostic, let
$\bar d(c)=H^{-1}\sum_h\tilde d_h(c)$ for the already fixed region.
We report $V_{\rm rate}$, the fraction of foils with $\bar d(f)>\bar d(t)$,
and $V_{\rm mass}=|\foils_t|^{-1}\sum_{f\in\foils_t}[\bar d(f)-\bar d(t)]_+$,
averaging over all foils, including zeros.
On COCO/OpenAI B/16, reranking at $\epsilon=.02$ changes $V_{\rm rate}$ from
$.18973$ to $.18751$ and $V_{\rm mass}$ from $.05878$ to $.05708$.
Violation breadth therefore also decreases descriptively.

\paragraph{Cases and uncertainty.}
Figure~\ref{fig:cases} retains archived selected cases, not frequencies.
All 59,302 rerun image--model records pass strategy/array checks.
CPU/MPS smoke checks cover only 1--2 images per setting.

\paragraph{Interpretation and scope.}
Rerun and archive are separate evidence sets. COCO B/16 image IDs align,
but three images change screen/failure status and ten change CCI region labels.
Some response changes exceed threshold rounding; frozen candidate tensors are
unavailable for causal attribution. COCO B/32 switches 614 times versus 613
in the archive, whose missing per-image fields prevent localization.
These differences are not attributed to MPS or treated as exact reproduction.
Inference is conditional on eligible images, templates, foils, candidates and
intervention. Restricted-foil conditional failure rates and robustness to new
candidate or intervention configurations remain untested.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
CCI-top1 auditing exposes specificity failures and sparse margin improvement.
Independent reruns support positive paired margin gains over Mean and Max-.1
under the tested protocol, with small gains over Max-.1 and measurable locality
costs. Target response, locality and sign repair remain distinct endpoints.
